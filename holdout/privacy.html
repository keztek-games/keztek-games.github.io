Holdout Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy applies to Holdout: Zombie Survival for Android, developed by KEZtek Undead Ventures LLC, Pennsylvania, USA.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not collect or share any data through the game. The game has no accounts, advertising or analytics. You do not need to create an account to play.

The game runs offline and has no internet access. Its only Android permission is vibrate.

Saved games

Your saves stay on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Uninstalling the game deletes those saves.

Google Play

Google Play handles your purchase and the free trial under Google's own privacy policy.

You can read Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13. The developer does not collect data from players.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact KEZtek Undead Ventures LLC at kiv.llc.services@gmail.com.
