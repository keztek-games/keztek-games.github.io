Holdout: Zombie Survival

Play classic zombie scenarios. Secure a building or scavenge an endless world.

Coming soon to Google Play

How long can you hold out while civilization falls?

Holdout: Zombie Survival is a single player, top-down pixel-art zombie survival game for Android phones. You search for supplies, board up doors and windows and dress your wounds. No one comes to rescue you. The point is how long you survive.

Search the world

Each new world is generated with towns, suburbs, farms, highways and gas stations. You can enter houses and stores to search for food, weapons, ammo, bandages, painkillers and beer.

You choose from three difficulty presets when you make a world, or set custom rules such as how many dead there are. Choose a Day Zero start to begin as the outbreak starts.

Watch the noise

Noise draws zombies. Gunshots carry far, while walls muffle sound. A horde can gather at a noise.

You can board up doors and windows. Zombies bang on doors and press on walls.

Make it through the night

Nights are dark. Your flashlight lights a cone in front of you. You get tired and need sleep.

The dead include shamblers, runners, crawlers, stalkers, screamers and bloaters. Human bandits also roam the world. They shout when they spot you.

You fight with melee weapons and guns. Guns need ammo and reloading. You can bleed and take gunshot wounds, and you dress your wounds as you try to stay alive.

When you die, the death screen shows Days Lived, Zombies Killed and Bandits Killed.

One purchase

Holdout costs US$4.99, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Google Play offers a 60-minute free trial before you buy.

You play offline. The game has no internet access at all, and its only Android permission is vibrate. You do not need an account.
